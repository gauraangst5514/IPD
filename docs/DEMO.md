# Demonstration guide — 30 October 2026

1. Sign in locally as assessor using private generated credentials.
2. Inspect the dashboard's actual capabilities and explicit limitations.
3. Search a provisioned research photograph in Photo Retrieval; explain similarity and candidate status.
4. Expand and toggle correspondence lines; inspect available damage/background diagnostics.
5. Open a synthetic claim and inspect vehicle/plate/TruFor/history evidence. A per-photo inconclusive result is different from an unavailable model.
6. Demonstrate approved gallery ingestion/duplicate detection and human review/PDF export.
7. Show the completed negative reranking and abstention results.

A local 102.6-second narrated walkthrough exists under `submission/`; it uses edited browser captures and synthesized narration, not a continuous take. Transcript/captions are included in the source publication; imagery/video stay local pending redistribution terms. The cloud build exists but its complete ML flow failed verification.
